📖 Past Perfect Continuous

Express a past action which started in the past and continued to happen after another action or time in the past.

✅ Form
Had been + Verb-ing
Positive: I had been working for hours.
Negative: She hadn't been sleeping well.
Question: Had you been waiting long?
🔹 Usage
Duration / Cause
  • Emphasize the duration of an activity — Richard needed a holiday because he had been working hard for six months.
  • Show the cause of a past action — I had been travelling all night, so I was tired.
  • Action that started in the past and continued to another past action — Sara had been working here for two weeks when she had the accident.
📊 Comparison
Past Perfect

I had finished my homework.

(Result / completed action)

Past Perfect Continuous

I had been doing my homework for 2 hours.

(Duration / ongoing before another action)

💡 More Examples
  • She had been sleeping for ten hours when I woke her.
  • They had been living in Beijing for three years when he lost his job.
  • I had to have a break. I had been driving so long.
  • His English was perfect. He had been studying it since he started school.
