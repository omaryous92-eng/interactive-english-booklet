📖 Grammar 1
Past Perfect
Form: had + past participle. Usage: actions completed before another past action. Time expressions: after, before, by the time, as soon as, when, until.
Rules + Examples
📖 Grammar 2
Past Perfect Continuous
Form: had been + verb-ing. Usage: actions that started in the past and continued up to another past action. Emphasis on duration or cause.
