🔗 Vocabulary 2: Idioms

Tap any card to flip it and see the definition + example. Learn at your own pace!

12 Idioms Common Expressions

💡 Idioms

jump for joy
be extremely happy
"I would jump for joy if I passed all my exams!"
take for granted
believe that sth is true without having any evidence
"Don't take your parents' support for granted, some kids don't have that."
drive crazy
make sb very angry
"That sound is really starting to drive me crazy!"
get stuck
be unable to move or continue
"I couldn't move forward in traffic because I got stuck in the jam."
push your way
push the people around you in order to get somewhere
"The plane was so full I had to push my way through the crowd."
on the edge of our seats
thrilled, very excited
"We were all on the edge of our seats during the final scene."
get under my skin
make sb upset, annoy or irritated
"That sound really gets under my skin – I can't stand it!"
lost for words
not know what to say because you are shocked or surprised
"I was completely lost for words after I tripped."
in agony
feeling a lot of pain
"He cried out in agony after falling off the bike."
with regret
a feeling of sadness about sth unpleasant
"She looked back at the choice she made, filled with regret."
burst into tears
suddenly start to cry
"After hearing the bad news, she burst into tears."
burst out laughing
suddenly start to laugh
"The joke was so funny I burst out laughing."
