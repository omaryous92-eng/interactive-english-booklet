7 Phrasal Verbs Prepositions

🔁 Phrasal Verbs

gone off
stop liking or being interested in sb
"I've completely gone off her after the way she treated me."
cheered up
make (someone) happier
"A call from her best friend really cheered her up."
chill out
relax without letting anything upset you
"After all that stress, you really need to chill out."
hit it off
become friends with sb immediately
"They immediately hit it off over their shared love of books."
fall out
argue with sb and stop being friends with them
"They used to be inseparable, but they suddenly fell out."
get down
make sb feel unhappy
"The repetitive tapping noise began to get me down."
stand up
not come to meet someone you had arranged to meet
"I waited for an hour, but she completely stood me up!"

📌 Prepositions

Lead TO
to result in or cause something
"Too much stress can lead to health problems."
Respond TO
to react to something
"She didn't respond to my message immediately."
Concentrate ON
to focus all your attention on something
"It's hard to concentrate on your work with all that noise."
Focus ON
to give attention to something
"We need to focus on the main topic."
Switch ON
to turn on (a device, machine, etc.)
"Don't forget to switch on the mic before you start talking."
Burst INTO
to suddenly start (crying, laughing, etc.)
"She burst into tears when she heard the bad news."
Figure OUT
to understand or solve something
"I couldn't figure out what was wrong with the computer."
Responsible FOR
having the duty of looking after something or someone
"He's the one responsible for organizing the event."
