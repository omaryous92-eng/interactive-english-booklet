📖 Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Usage, signal words, stative verbs, and more.

🔹 Present Simple
Habits & Facts

Usage

  • Habits, regular actions
  • Facts, permanent situations
  • Stative verbs (see, hear, think, etc.)
  • Future timetabled events

Examples

  • I wash my hair every day.
  • Water boils at 100 degrees.
  • I have one brother.
  • The train leaves at 4.
Signal words: always, never, often, sometimes, every day, once a month, etc.
🔸 Present Continuous
Now & Temporary

Usage

  • Actions in progress now
  • Temporary actions
  • Changing/developing situations
  • Future arrangements

Examples

  • I'm brushing my teeth.
  • I'm living with my cousins until I find an apartment.
  • The climate is changing quickly.
  • I'm meeting John this evening.
Signal words: now, at the moment, these days, this week/month, etc.
⚠️ Stative Verbs (not usually used in continuous)
  • senses: hear, see, smell, look, seem, sound
  • opinion: believe, consider, like, love, hate, prefer, think
  • possession: have, own, belong
  • others: agree, be, depend, need, mean, remember, realise, recognise, want
