📊 Comparison: Present Simple vs Present Continuous

✅ Present Simple
  • Habits — regular actions or situations
  • Facts — permanent situations or states
  • Stative verbs — see, hear, think, like, etc.
  • Future timetabled events
"I wash my hair every day."
"Water boils at 100 degrees."
"The train leaves at 4."
Signal words:
always never often sometimes every day once a month
🔄 Present Continuous
  • Actions in progress — happening now / around now
  • Temporary actions — this week, this month
  • Changing situations — developing over time
  • Future arrangements — planned events
"I'm brushing my teeth."
"I'm living with my cousins until I find an apartment."
"I'm meeting John this evening."
Signal words:
now at the moment these days this week/month
⚠️ Stative Verbs (not usually used in continuous)

These verbs describe states, not actions. They are normally used in the Present Simple.

👁️ Senses hear, see, smell, look, seem, sound
💭 Opinion believe, consider, like, love, hate, prefer, think
📦 Possession have, own, belong
🤝 Other agree, be, depend, need, mean, remember, realise, recognise, want

✅ Correct: "I understand the lesson." (not "I am understanding")
✅ Correct: "This soup tastes delicious." (not "is tasting")

📌 Quick Reference Card
Present Simple
  • 📅 Habits & routines
  • 🌍 Facts & permanent states
  • 🧠 Stative verbs
  • 🚂 Timetabled future
Present Continuous
  • ⏳ Actions in progress now
  • 📆 Temporary situations
  • 📈 Changing situations
  • 📋 Future arrangements
