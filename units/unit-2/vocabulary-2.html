🔗 Idioms & Collocations

Natural combinations of words that frequently appear together.

💡 Idioms

dates back
have existed for a particular length of time
"The tradition dates back to the 12th century."
identify with
feel that you are similar to sb and be able to understand how they feel
"Many teenagers identify with celebrities who share their personal struggles."
look back at
think about what happened in the past
"The director looked back at her early days in the film industry."
spread out over
make sth happen over a longer period of time
"The campaign was spread out over three months."
call off
decide not to do sth that you had planned (Syn: cancel)
"They had to call off the outdoor concert."
go ahead
start to do sth after sb has given you permission
"We can go ahead with the construction plans."
talk into
persuade sb to do sth
"He tried to talk his brother into investing in the risky project."
dress up
put on special clothes
"The children were excited to dress up as their favorite storybook characters."

🔹 Collocations

A collocation is a natural combination of words that frequently appear together.

give a speech
deliver a formal talk to an audience
"The mayor will give a speech during the opening ceremony."
play a role
participate actively in sth
"Volunteers played a role in organizing the fundraiser."
build a reputation
become known for sth over time
"She built a reputation as a passionate advocate."
widely known
famous or recognized by many people
"The author is widely known for her unique storytelling style."
hold a festival
organize and run a public celebration
"The town holds a festival every spring."
have roots in
originate from
"The ceremony has roots in ancient rituals."
use without permission
use sth without asking for approval
"Using someone's artwork without permission is a violation."
