⏳ Used to / Would

Past habits, states, and adaptation.

🔹 Used to + base form
Past habits & states
Positive: I used to have long hair.
Negative: We didn't use to argue.
Question: Did you use to drink so much?
  • Used for past habits (actions that happened repeatedly but don't happen now)
  • Used for past states (situations that were true but are no longer true)
  • Not used with specific time references (e.g., not "I used to go yesterday")
I used to play football when I was young. (habit)
She used to live in Cairo. (state)
🔸 Would + base form
Past habits (only)
Positive: We would go to the park every Sunday.
  • Used for past habits (repeated actions in the past)
  • NOT used for states (e.g., not "I would have long hair")
  • Often used with nostalgic or memorable feelings
When I was a child, I would climb trees. (habit)
He would always hum while working. (habit)
🔄 Be / Get used to + noun / -ing
Be used to – familiar / comfortable with
Teachers are used to long working days.
Get used to – becoming familiar / adapting
I'm getting used to waking up early.
⚠️ Note: "used to" (past habit) is different from "be used to" (familiar) and "get used to" (adapting).
