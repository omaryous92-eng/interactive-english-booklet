📊 Comparison 1: Past Simple vs Past Continuous

✅ Past Simple
Form: V2 (regular: -ed / irregular: 2nd column)
  • Completed actions — finished in the past
  • Past habits or facts
  • Sequential actions — one after another
"I watched a film last night."
"He always walked to school."
"She opened the door and went inside."
Signal words:
yesterday last week/month/year (days) ago when
🔄 Past Continuous
Form: was/were + verb-ing
  • Action in progress — at a specific time in the past
  • Background action — interrupted by another action
  • Two ongoing actions — at the same time
"I was reading a book when the phone rang."
"She was cooking while he was watching TV."
"At 8 PM, I was having dinner."
Signal words:
while as at (time)

📊 Comparison 2: Past Simple vs Present Perfect

✅ Past Simple
  • Finished past actions — with a specific time
  • No connection to the present
  • Sequential past events
"I saw that movie last year."
"She lived in Paris for 5 years." (she no longer lives there)
"He called me yesterday."
Signal words:
yesterday last week in 2010 ago
⏳ Present Perfect
  • Past action with present result
  • Life experiences (ever, never)
  • Unfinished actions (for, since)
  • Recent events (just, already, yet)
"I have seen that movie." (I know it now)
"She has lived in Paris for 5 years." (she still lives there)
"He has just called me."
Signal words:
just already yet for since ever never
📌 Quick Reference Card
Past Simple
  • ✅ Completed actions
  • 📅 Past habits
  • 📜 Sequential events
  • ⏰ Specific time
Past Continuous
  • ⏳ Actions in progress
  • ⚡ Interrupted actions
  • 🔄 Simultaneous actions
  • ⏰ At a specific time
Present Perfect
  • 📌 Present result
  • ⭐ Life experiences
  • 🔄 Unfinished actions
  • 📰 Recent events
