📖 Past Simple vs Past Continuous

Completed actions vs ongoing actions in the past.

✅ Past Simple
Completed
Form: V2 (regular: -ed / irregular: 2nd column)
I played / She went

Usage

  • Completed actions in the past
  • Past habits or facts
  • Sequential actions

Examples

  • I watched a film last night.
  • He always walked to school.
  • She opened the door and went inside.
Signal words: yesterday, last (week/month/year), (days/months) ago, when
🔄 Past Continuous
Ongoing
Form: was/were + verb-ing
I was playing / They were eating

Usage

  • Action in progress at a specific time
  • Background action interrupted by another
  • Two ongoing actions at the same time

Examples

  • I was reading a book when the phone rang.
  • She was cooking while he was watching TV.
  • At 8 PM, I was having dinner.
Signal words: while, as, at (time)
📊 Comparison
Past Simple

She washed the dishes.

(Finished action)

Past Continuous

She was washing the dishes when the phone rang.

(Ongoing action interrupted)
