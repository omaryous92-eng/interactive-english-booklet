📚 Vocabulary 1

Tap any card to flip it and see the definition + example.

53 words Global Culture
global
relating to the whole world
"Climate change is a global problem that affects every country."
culture
the common habits and beliefs of a particular group of people
"Egyptian culture is rich in history and traditions."
server
a person who serves food
"The server brought our meals quickly and politely."
funeral
a ceremony that takes place when sb's dead body is buried
"Many people attended his funeral to say goodbye."
identity
the things that make you different from others
"Music is an important part of my identity."
lifestyle
sb's way of living
"A healthy lifestyle includes exercise and good food."
reflect
show or express the nature of sth
"His actions reflect his kindness."
tribe
a group of related families who live in the same area and share a culture
"The tribe has lived in the desert for many generations."
lead
live (a particular type of life)
"She leads a busy life as a student and athlete."
sensibly
in a way that is logical and practical
"He sensibly decided to stay home during the storm."
bead
a small, round piece (usually of glass, plastic or wood) with a hole through it
"She made a bracelet using colorful beads."
head wrap / turban
a head covering made of cloth wound round the head
"He wore a turban as part of his traditional clothing."
kilt
a skirt that men in Scotland traditionally wear
"He wore a kilt during the cultural festival."
tartan
a pattern of coloured squares and lines on cloth, typical of Scotland
"His scarf had a red and green tartan design."
warrior
skilled fighter
"The ancient warrior protected his village bravely."
form
type, kind
"This form of music is very popular among teenagers."
wearer
sb who wears sth
"The wearer of the ring must be very careful with it."
status
sb's position in a social group
"Teachers have a high status in many societies."
inspire
give sb a creative idea
"Her story inspired me to become a writer."
trademark
register a name or design to protect it from illegal copying
"The company trademarked its logo to protect it."
fashion house
a company that designs and sells clothes, shoes and accessories
"The fashion house launched a new summer collection."
permission
the act of allowing sb to do sth
"You need permission to leave the classroom."
sea shell
the hard outer covering that protects some sea creatures
"She collected a beautiful sea shell on the beach."
series
a number of events or things that are similar or related
"I watched a series of documentaries about space."
hold
have an event, e.g., a sports event, a meeting, an election, etc.
"The school will hold a science fair next week."
state
say or write sth in a clear and careful way
"He stated that he was not involved in the incident."
settle
go to live in a new place
"Many families settled in the city after moving from villages."
plaid
cloth with a pattern of lines and squares
"He wore a shirt made of red plaid."
originally
in the beginning
"The town was originally a small fishing village."
impressive
important or very large
"The athlete gave an impressive performance."
astonishing
very surprising
"It was astonishing to see how fast he ran."
massive
very large
"The stadium was a massive building filled with fans."
store
keep sth in a special place so that you can use it in future
"We store food in the fridge to keep it fresh."
pierced
with a small hole used for jewellery
"She has pierced ears and wears earrings."
pirate
a person who sails in a ship and attacks and steals from other ships
"The pirate searched for hidden treasure."
equator
the line around the middle of the Earth
"Countries near the equator are usually very hot."
proper
suitable, correct
"You should use proper grammar in formal writing."
wax
the substance used to make candles
"The candle is made of wax."
plug
fill a hole with suitable material
"He used clay to plug the hole in the wall."
cannon
a large gun on wheels that fired heavy metal balls
"The castle displayed an old cannon."
parade
a large number of people walking in an organised way to celebrate a special occasion
"We watched a parade on National Day."
tradition
a very old belief, principle or way of acting
"It is a tradition to eat special food during holidays."
spectator
sb who watches a sports event or other activity without taking part
"The spectators cheered loudly during the match."
spectacle
a public event that is exciting to watch
"The fireworks were a beautiful spectacle."
costume
a set of clothes worn in a particular country or for a special activity
"She wore a traditional costume for the festival."
mask
a covering for the face that protects or hides it
"He wore a mask to protect himself from dust."
festivities
parties and other activities that celebrate a special occasion
"The city was full of festivities during Eid."
legend
a very old story, usually untrue, that people talk about a famous event or person
"The legend tells of a hidden treasure in the mountain."
bonfire
large fire that you burn outdoors
"We gathered around a bonfire at night."
ritual
a series of actions performed as part of a ceremony
"The ritual included lighting candles."
come of age
reach the age when you are considered an adult
"He will come of age when he turns eighteen."
role
the position or purpose that sb has in an organisation or in a relationship
"Musicians play an important role in our culture."
ceremony
a set of traditional actions performed on a special social or religious occasion
"The wedding ceremony was very beautiful."
