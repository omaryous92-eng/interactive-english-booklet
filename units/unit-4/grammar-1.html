📖 Future Tenses

Expressing the future: will, going to, present continuous, future continuous, future perfect, and time clauses.

🔹 will + base form
Spontaneous / Predictions
I'll open the door for you. (spontaneous)
It will rain tomorrow. (prediction)
🔸 be going to + base form
Plans / Predictions with evidence
We are going to travel to Italy. (plan)
Look at those clouds – it's going to rain. (evidence)
🔹 Present Continuous
Arrangements
I'm meeting John this evening. (fixed arrangement)
🔸 Future Continuous
will be + verb-ing
This time tomorrow, I will be flying to Spain. (action in progress at a specific future time)
🔹 Future Perfect
will have + past participle
By next year, she will have travelled for 7 years. (completed by a future time)
⏰ Future Time Clauses

Use present tense (not future) after time conjunctions: when, as soon as, until, after, before, by the time

  • As soon as I finish school, I will go to work.
  • I will study English until I find a good job.
  • When I have some free time, I'll visit Niagara Falls.
  • After I finish my presentation, I'll feel happy.
