🏙️ Vocabulary: City Living

Tap any card to flip it and see the definition + example. Learn at your own pace!

56 words 11 idioms

📚 Vocabulary Words

rural
in the countryside (Opp: urban)
"My grandparents live in a rural village surrounded by farms."
commercial
related to buying and selling
"The city center is a commercial area full of shops and offices."
identical
exactly the same
"The twins were wearing identical jackets."
rapid
fast
"There has been a rapid increase in the number of cars in the city."
resident
a person who has their home in a place
"Every resident of the building received a safety notice."
block of flats
a large building divided into apartments
"My friend lives in a block of flats near the school."
pace
the speed at which sth happens
"Technology is changing at a fast pace."
shortage
a situation where there isn't enough of sth needed (Syn: lack)
"There was a water shortage during the hot summer."
entertainment
films, music, shows, etc. that help people have fun
"Watching movies is a popular form of entertainment."
commute
travel between work and home regularly
"My father commutes to work by train every day."
outskirts
the area around a town or city
"They built a new hospital on the outskirts of the city."
inhabitant
a person or animal that lives in a place
"The camel is a common inhabitant of desert regions."
historical
connected with the past or with the study of the past
"We visited a historical museum to learn about the past."
devote
give time, attention or energy to sth
"She devotes two hours a day to studying."
walkable
suitable for walking
"The neighborhood is very walkable, with many shops nearby."
pedestrian
a person who is walking
"The pedestrian waited for the traffic light to turn green."
housing
buildings for people to live in
"The government is building new housing for families."
linear
continuing logically from one part to the next
"The story follows a linear timeline from beginning to end."
approach
a way of doing sth or thinking about sth
"We need a different approach to solve this problem."
growth
an increase in the size or the importance of sth
"The city has experienced significant growth in recent years."
residential
having mainly private houses, not offices or factories
"This is a quiet residential area with many houses."
reduce
make or become smaller (Syn: decrease)
"We should reduce plastic waste to protect the environment."
get around
travel to different places
"Many students use bicycles to get around the city."
expand
make sth bigger
"The company plans to expand its offices next year."
modernist
related to the modern art movement
"The building has a modernist design with simple shapes."
propose
suggest a plan for other people to think about
"The mayor proposed a new public park."
raise
lift to a higher position
"Please raise your hand if you know the answer."
spread
reach or cover a wider area
"News of the event quickly spread across the town."
claim
say that sth is true without being able to prove it
"Some people claim that the old house is haunted."
end up
reach a particular place or situation after completing other activities
"If you miss the bus, you may end up arriving late."
humanistic
treating people with respect, making sure they are happy, safe and healthy
"The school follows a humanistic approach."
course
the direction in which sth moves
"The river changed its course after the heavy rain."
continually
without a break (Syn: continuously)
"The machine ran continually throughout the day."
uninviting
not attractive or pleasant (Opp: inviting)
"The old building looked dark and uninviting."
demand
ask for sth forcefully
"The workers demanded better working conditions."
fence
a barrier between two areas of land, usually made of wood or wire
"A wooden fence surrounds the garden."
unfenced
not surrounded by a fence (Opp: fenced)
"The field is unfenced, so animals can enter easily."
low-rise
not tall, with only a few floors (Opp: high-rise)
"We live in a low-rise apartment building with three floors."
suburb
an area where people live that is outside the city centre
"Many families move to the suburbs for a quieter lifestyle."
district
an area of a town or country that has a particular purpose
"The business district is crowded during the day."
industrial
related to factories
"The city has a large industrial area with many factories."
estate
a group of houses or factories built according to a plan
"They moved into a new housing estate last year."
bike lane
a part of the road that can only be used by people riding bicycles
"Cyclists should stay in the bike lane for safety."
pavement
a path on the side of the road that people can walk on
"We walked along the pavement instead of the road."
crossroads
a place where two roads meet and cross each other
"The accident happened at a busy crossroads."
roadworks
repair or building work on a road
"The traffic was slow because of roadworks."
junction
the place where two or more roads or railways meet
"Turn left at the next junction."
pedestrian crossing
a place in a road where traffic must stop so that pedestrians can cross
"Always use the pedestrian crossing when crossing a busy road."
pedestrian zone
an area where no vehicles are allowed
"Cars are not allowed in the pedestrian zone."
speed camera
a camera at the side of the road that takes pictures of cars that are going too fast
"A speed camera caught the driver speeding."
speed limit
the fastest you are allowed to drive in a specific area
"The speed limit on this road is 60 km/h."
facilities
buildings, equipment and services available for a particular purpose
"The sports center has excellent facilities for students."
venue
the place where an event or meeting takes place
"The school hall was the venue for the graduation ceremony."
urbanisation
the process in which towns and cities are built where there was countryside before
"Urbanisation has led to the growth of many cities."
boundary
a line that marks the limit of sth
"A wall marks the boundary between the two properties."
ghost town
a town that used to be busy but now has few or no people
"The mining town became a ghost town after the mine closed."

💡 Idioms

live on top of each other
live very close to each other
"In many modern cities, people have to live on top of each other."
resist the temptation
stop yourself from doing sth you like but shouldn't do
"I couldn't resist the temptation to eat some more chocolate cake."
have the best of both worlds
enjoy the advantages of two very different things at the same time
"He has the best of both worlds."
from all walks of life
with different jobs or positions in society
"There are people from all walks of life in my dance class."
on the increase
getting larger
"His book collection is always on the increase."
take into consideration
think about sth before making a decision
"They need to take the local area into consideration."
the talk of the town
what everyone is talking about
"It was the talk of the town last year."
the urban jungle
city life (especially if you think it's unpleasant)
"The urban jungle has become unbearable."
go to town
do sth with energy and spending a lot on it
"She really went to town on her birthday party."
all over the place
not tidy or not well organised
"Your work is all over the place."
life in the fast lane
a busy and exciting way of life
"She used to enjoy life in the fast lane."
