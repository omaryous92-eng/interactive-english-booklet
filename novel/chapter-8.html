📖 Chapter 8: Darnay's Journey to Paris

Darnay receives a letter and travels to France.

📝 Summary
Chapters 7 & 8
  • Six years of turmoil in France and peace in the Manette-Darnay household passed.
  • Tellson's Bank in London became the news center and gathering place for all noblemen who had sent their money to England before the revolution.
  • At Tellson's office in Paris, there was fear that the angry mob would break in and destroy important papers. Mr. Lorry would be sent from England to Paris for that mission.
  • Darnay received an unopened letter addressed to "The Marquis St. Evremonde, of France" — Darnay's real name.
  • The letter was from Gabelle who wrote that he was in prison and would surely be killed if Darnay didn't come to Paris to clarify his case.
  • Darnay's journey to Paris was difficult. He was stopped for tens of times every day. In the middle of the night, he was awakened by guards and told he would be sent to prison.
❓ Questions

  • Why was Darnay's journey to Paris difficult?
  • What was Mr. Lorry's mission in Paris?
