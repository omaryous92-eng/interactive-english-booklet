🎯 Themes & Symbols

🔹 Major Themes
Analysis

1. Resurrection

Dr. Manette is "recalled to life" after 18 years in prison. Charles Darnay escapes the guillotine twice. Sydney Carton undergoes spiritual resurrection before his death, finding purpose and redemption through sacrifice.

2. Self-Sacrifice and Selfishness

Villains are distinguished by their selfish cruelty (the Evremonde brothers, Madame Defarge). Heroes are defined by their willingness to sacrifice themselves for others (Sydney Carton, Dr. Manette).

3. Revolution and Retribution

Dickens provides a vivid description of the Reign of Terror, portraying it as the inevitable outcome of years of tyranny by the French aristocracy. The novel warns against the dangers of mob rule and vengeance.

🔸 Symbols
Meaning
The Broken Wine Cask

Represents both blood and hunger — foreshadows the bloodshed of the revolution and symbolizes the desperation of the French people.

The Guillotine

Symbol of both justice and horror. Becomes a tool of mass murder during the Revolution, representing the loss of moral direction.

Madame Defarge's Knitting

Symbol of fate and vengeance. She knits the names of people to be executed, reflecting how personal hatred fuels political violence.

Shadows and Darkness

Suggest secrecy, fear, and danger — representing the looming threat of violence or emotional torment.
