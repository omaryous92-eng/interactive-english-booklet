👤 Main Characters

The key figures in Dickens' A Tale of Two Cities.

Doctor Alexandre Manette
Protagonist

A French physician who was unjustly imprisoned in the Bastille for 18 years. He is "recalled to life" after his release and forms a close bond with his daughter, Lucie. His past trauma resurfaces, but he ultimately finds redemption.

Lucie Manette
Healing / Hope

The daughter of Dr. Manette. She represents love, compassion, and healing. Her devotion to her father and husband guides the family through their struggles. She is a source of strength and hope for those around her.

Charles Darnay
Aristocrat turned teacher

A French aristocrat who renounces his title and wealth to live an honest life in England. He is arrested multiple times during the revolution and is saved by Sydney Carton's ultimate sacrifice.

Sydney Carton
Redemption / Sacrifice

A brilliant but dissolute lawyer who looks exactly like Charles Darnay. He confesses his love to Lucie but knows he is unworthy. He ultimately sacrifices his life to save Darnay, achieving spiritual redemption.

Madame Thérèse Defarge
Vengeance

A ruthless revolutionary who knits the names of those condemned to death. She represents the destructive power of hatred and revenge, driven by the murder of her family by the Evremonde brothers.

Monsieur Ernest Defarge
Revolutionary leader

A wine-shop owner in Saint Antoine and a leader of the revolution. He is more measured than his wife, showing some compassion for Dr. Manette.
