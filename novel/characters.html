👤 Main Characters

The key figures in Dickens' A Tale of Two Cities. Hover over each card to learn more about their role and relationships.

🏛️
Mr. Jarvis Lorry
Elderly Banker

An elderly banker at Tellson's Bank who befriends and helps the Manettes. He reunites Doctor Manette with Lucie and brings him to England. He employs Jerry Cruncher as a porter.

Loyal Protector Tellson's Bank
⚰️
Jerry Cruncher
Porter & Grave Robber

A porter for Tellson's Bank who also works as a secret grave robber. He is a rough, superstitious man who provides comic relief but also demonstrates moral complexity.

Complex Comic Relief Grave Robber
🍷
Ernest Defarge
Revolutionary Leader

A wine-shop owner and leader of the revolution. He is a revolutionary with a conscience, formerly a servant of Doctor Manette. He is married to Madame Defarge and struggles with his wife's extreme vengeance.

Revolutionary Conscience Wine-shop
🧶
Madame Thérèse Defarge
The Knitter of Fate

Hardened by her hatred of the Evrémonde family, she is the embodiment of vengeance. She knits the names of those condemned to death. Her relentless pursuit of revenge drives the revolution's bloodiest acts.

Vengeance Revolutionary Knitter
🩺
Doctor Alexandre Manette
Former Bastille Prisoner

Secretly imprisoned for 18 years in the Bastille, he is "recalled to life" by his daughter Lucie. His mental health is restored by her love. He unknowingly denounces his son-in-law, Charles Darnay.

Resurrection Father Prisoner
👩‍🦰
Miss Pross
Loyal Companion

A devoted servant who has dedicated her life to caring for Lucie. She is fiercely protective and ultimately kills Madame Defarge in a struggle, saving Lucie's family.

Loyal Protector Devoted
👨‍⚖️
Charles Darnay
Born Charles St. Evrémonde

A French aristocrat who renounces his title to live an honest life in England. He marries Lucie Manette. He is arrested during the Revolution and saved by Sydney Carton's sacrifice.

Renounced Husband Aristocrat
🌟
Lucie Manette
The Golden Thread

The moral and emotional center of the book. She is devoted to her father, Doctor Manette, and her husband, Charles Darnay. Her love and compassion heal those around her.

Compassion Healer Golden Thread
🍸
Sydney Carton
The Redeemed Alcoholic

An alcoholic lawyer who finds redemption through his death. He loves Lucie Manette but knows he is unworthy. He sacrifices his life to save Charles Darnay, achieving spiritual resurrection.

Redemption Sacrifice Resurrection
