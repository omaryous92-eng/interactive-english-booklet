📖 Chapter 9: Darnay in Prison

Darnay is imprisoned at La Force and Dr. Manette tries to save him.

📝 Summary
Chapters 9 & 10

Darnay arrived in France to save his servant Gabelle but he could travel very slowly towards Paris. He had to wait for a long time while officials carefully read his papers. When the officials saw Gabelle's letter and knew that Darnay was the Marquis, they decided to send him to the prison of La Force.

After the revolution, the revolutionaries came to abuse their power. They sent anyone from the aristocrats to the prison of La Force or even killed him without trials or laws just under the name of freedom. Although some of them were innocent and had never caused harm to anyone.

Dr. Manette's years as a prisoner in the Bastille had become a source of strength rather than weakness. He became a hero and walked through the mob unharmed. They went on saying "Long live Bastille prisoner! Help his friend in the Bastille." So, Dr. Manette was able to save his daughter's husband just as Lucie had saved him before.
