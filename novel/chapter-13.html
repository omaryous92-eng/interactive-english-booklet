📖 Chapter 13: Carton's Sacrifice

Sydney Carton saves Darnay and gives his life.

📝 Summary
Chapters 13 & 14
  • Darnay was alone in his cell with no hope of escape.
  • At one o'clock, the door was opened, and he was surprised to see Carton face to face. Carton ordered him to take off his boots and wear Carton's.
  • Carton asked Darnay to write a letter to Lucie. Carton was fulfilling his promise to her. He came behind Darnay and drugged him.
  • Quickly, Carton wore Darnay's clothes and called Barsad to enter. The spy presented himself. Carton asked the spy to get help and carry Darnay as if he were Carton to the coach.
  • Carton went to the guillotine. A dressmaker who once met Darnay at La Force came close to Carton and asked to hold his hands. She realized that he wasn't Darnay. He told her he was replacing the prisoner for the sake of his wife and child.
🔄 M. Defarge and His Wife Disagree

M. Defarge said that they had to stop taking any more revenge but his wife said, "Tell the Wind and Fire to stop but don't tell me." Then M. Defarge told them that the poor family that was so cruelly treated by the two Evremonde brothers was the family of his wife. She was the little sister of the boy who was killed and the girl who died. The husband was her sister's husband, the father was her father.
