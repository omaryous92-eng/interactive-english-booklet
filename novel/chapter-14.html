📖 Chapter 14: The Final Scene

Carton goes to the guillotine and the family escapes.

📝 Summary
Chapters 13 & 14
  • Mr. Lorry, Lucie, and Dr. Manette left Paris. Darnay was still unconscious. Papers were examined at the borders. Then they were allowed to continue their journey.
  • When Miss Pross returned home, she found Madame Defarge who asked her about Lucie. Miss Pross didn't want Madame Defarge to know that Lucie had left.
  • Miss Pross took hold of Madame Defarge around the waist. Madame Defarge scratched Miss Pross's face. Madame Defarge tried to shoot Miss Pross, but she killed herself by mistake.
  • Sydney Carton and other prisoners were taken by tumbrels to the guillotine. Carton spent his last few moments thinking of the people he was leaving behind.
💭 Carton's Final Words

"It is a far, far better thing that I do, than I have ever done; it is a far, far better rest that I go to, than I have ever known."
