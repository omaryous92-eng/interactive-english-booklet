📚 Introduction to A Tale of Two Cities

📖 Novel Overview
Charles Dickens
  • Written by: Charles Dickens
  • Type of Work: Novel
  • Genres: Historical fiction; political commentary
  • First Published: In weekly installments in All the Year Round, from April 30 to November 29, 1859
  • Setting: London and Paris, 1775–1792
🏛️ Social Background
Class Injustice

The novel takes place in two cities: London and Paris. In both cities, there was unrest and uneasiness. The society was divided into two classes:

Upper Class (Aristocrats)

Represented by kings, queens, and aristocrats. They lived in luxury and comfort, having complete rights and disregarding the lower class.

Lower Class (The Mob)

Represented by poor peasants and common people. They lived in poverty, misery, and hunger, suffering from high taxes and unjust laws.

These conditions burst into revolution in 1789 — the French Revolution, calling for "Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity".

📜 Historical Background

Dickens was heavily influenced by Thomas Carlyle's The French Revolution: A History (1837). While Dickens sympathized with the suffering of the French poor, he also warned against how easily the oppressed could become oppressors once power shifted. The novel warns against the dangers of social injustice, mob rule, and vengeance.

The dual settings of London and Paris allowed Dickens to draw comparisons between the relative stability of England and the turmoil in revolutionary France.
