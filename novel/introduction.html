📖 A Tale of Two Cities

"It was the best of times, it was the worst of times..."
A journey through love, sacrifice, and revolution in London and Paris.

📅 1775–1793 🇬🇧 London 🇫🇷 Paris ✍️ Charles Dickens
⬇️ Scroll down to explore the story timeline ⬇️

⏳ The Story Timeline

Follow the key events of the novel in chronological order

🏛️
The Opening

"It was the best of times, it was the worst of times."
Jarvis Lorry travels to Paris with Lucie Manette to find her father, Doctor Alexandre Manette, who has been released after 18 years in the Bastille.

1775
🩺
The Reunion

Lucie meets her father for the first time. Doctor Manette has forgotten his name and only remembers his prison number: "One Hundred and Five North Tower." Lucie's love begins to heal him.

1775
⚖️
The Trial

Charles Darnay is accused of treason. Sydney Carton, who looks exactly like Darnay, helps save him. This is the first encounter between Darnay and Carton.

1780
❤️
Love & Marriage

Charles Darnay marries Lucie Manette. Carton confesses his love to Lucie but knows he is unworthy. He makes a promise: he would give his life to save someone she loves.

1781
🗡️
The Marquis's Death

The cruel Marquis St. Evrémonde runs over a child with his carriage. Gaspard, the child's father, murders the Marquis in revenge. The revolution is brewing.

1781
🇫🇷
The Revolution Begins

The storming of the Bastille in 1789 marks the start of the French Revolution. The Defarges lead the people in a wave of violence and destruction.

1789
📜
Darnay's Arrest

Darnay returns to France to save his servant Gabelle. He is arrested and imprisoned at La Force. Doctor Manette uses his influence to get Darnay a trial.

1792
🧶
The Letter from the Bastille

Doctor Manette's secret letter is produced in court. It reveals the cruelty of the Evrémonde brothers and condemns Darnay to death. Madame Defarge's revenge is complete.

1793
🕊️
Carton's Sacrifice

Sydney Carton takes Darnay's place in prison and goes to the guillotine. His final words: "It is a far, far better thing that I do, than I have ever done."

1793

🏛️ Social Background

The society in both cities was divided into two classes

👑

Upper Class (Aristocrats)

Represented by kings, queens, and aristocrats. They lived in luxury and comfort, having complete rights and disregarding the lower class.

👑 Privileged
🌾

Lower Class (The Mob)

Represented by poor peasants and common people. They lived in poverty, misery, and hunger, suffering from high taxes and unjust laws.

💪 Oppressed
