📖 Chapter 7: The Revolution Begins

The Defarges and the cycle of violence.

📝 Summary
Chapters 7 & 8
  • Both Monsieur and Madame Defarge were leaders. Monsieur Defarge focused on organizing the Jacques while Madame Defarge kept recording the names of people who were condemned to death in her list.
  • Monsieur Defarge valued fairness and justice. Madame Defarge showed no concern for other people's lives; she only cared about murder and revenge.
  • Defarge reported to his wife news from his friend Jacques in the police. A new spy had been sent to Saint Antoine — John Barsad. Defarge gave the spy's name and description to his wife to put him in her list of death.
  • The Defarges were childless and considered the revolution as their child.
🔄 The Cycle of Violence and Revenge

Gaspard's horrible death showed the cycle of violence that was rising when the Marquis killed Gaspard's son, then Gaspard killed the Marquis, then Gaspard was hung. As a result, Madame Defarge added all the Evremonde family to her list of revenge, and the revolutionaries burnt their castle.

The revolution was a result of oppression that the mobs had suffered from, as they lived a miserable poor life, getting low wages, starving, and living under the tyranny of the aristocratic class.
