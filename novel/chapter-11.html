📖 Chapter 11: The Letter from the Bastille

Dr. Manette's letter condemns the Evremonde family.

📝 Summary
Chapters 11 & 12
  • While Miss Pross and Jerry were shopping, Miss Pross saw her brother, John Barsad (Solomon). He was an official there and she was putting him in great danger.
  • Mr. Lorry was sitting by the fire when he suddenly saw Sydney Carton entering the room with John Barsad.
  • Carton knew everything about Barsad. Barsad was beaten and had no choice but to give in. Carton asked him if he could pass in and out of the prison whenever he wanted, and Barsad said he could.
  • The court room was crowded. Charles was accused of being a noble. They said that his family was one of the cruelest of all the noble families.
  • Defarge produced a letter written years earlier by Dr. Manette in prison condemning all Evremondes for the murder of Madame Defarge's family and for imprisoning the Doctor.
  • Based on this evidence, the court sentenced Darnay to death.
📜 Dr. Manette's Letter

In his letter, Dr. Manette told the story of how the Evremonde brothers had tortured a woman and her brother. The doctor ended his letter by laying a curse on the twin brothers to the last of the race.

"I, Alexander Manette, pray that these two men will die a painful death and that their whole race will be totally destroyed."
