📖 Chapter 4: Darnay's Acquittal

The trial concludes and Darnay is freed.

📝 Summary
Acquittal

The trial of Charles Darnay continued. Questions from Darnay's attorney, Mr. Stryver, indicated that Cly and Barsad were the real spies. However, the turning point in the trial occurred when Sydney Carton, Stryver's assistant, pointed out that Carton and Darnay looked alike enough to be doubles. Therefore, the court acquitted Darnay.

After the trial, Darnay, Carton, and Stryver began spending time at the Manette home, obviously attracted to Lucie's beauty and kind nature.
