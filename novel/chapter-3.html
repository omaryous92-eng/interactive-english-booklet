📖 Chapter 3: The Trial

Charles Darnay is accused of treason and Sydney Carton saves him.

📝 Summary
Chapters 3 & 4

Charles Darnay was accused of being a spy and he was very close to certain death after a red-haired man said he saw Darnay in a town that has ships and soldiers.

Fate played an important role to save his life, as there seemed a man who looked exactly like Darnay in the same room — Sydney Carton. Sydney Carton was his lawyer's assistant and he was the one who wrote on the paper what Stryver said. Sydney Carton was the one who came up with the idea to save Darnay and bring him back to life after he was very close to death.

👤 Darnay vs Carton — Double Mirror Image
Charles Darnay
  • Polite, organized
  • A real gentleman
  • Insisted on thanking Carton after the trial
  • Invited Carton to the restaurant
Sydney Carton
  • Ill-mannered, careless
  • Impolitely behaved
  • Used to drink heavily
  • His way of life was the reason of being a failure

Note: Darnay and Carton looked exactly like each other but that was only in their physical appearance. However, they were completely different in their characters.
