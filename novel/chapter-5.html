📖 Chapter 5: The Marquis Evremonde

The cruelty of the aristocracy and the death of a child.

📝 Summary
Chapters 5 & 6
  • The Marquis of Evremonde's carriage passed over Gaspard's child and killed him.
  • M. Defarge tried to comfort the poor father saying that it was much better for the child to die once without pain than to live a life full of pain, misery, and poverty.
  • The Marquis was angry and blamed the people for being careless. He was more concerned about his horses than the poor child he had killed.
  • A coin came flying back into the carriage. The Marquis ordered the driver to stop and said if he knew who threw that coin, he would have him crushed under the wheels.
  • Darnay disapproved of the deeds of his family. He opposed the cruel and unjust way in which they treated the poor people.
  • The Marquis Evremonde was found killed in his bed with a knife driven into his heart. Around the handle was a piece of paper: "Drive him fast to his tomb. This from Jacques."
  • Gaspard killed the Marquis for passing over his child earlier that day.
❓ Questions

  • "Why you people cannot take care of your children, I hope my horses are not hurt." Comment referring to the theme within.
  • Darnay washed his hands of his family's actions. Discuss.
