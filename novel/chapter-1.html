📖 Chapter 1

"It was the best of times, it was the worst of times..."

📝 Summary
Opening

It was the best of times, it was the worst of times — Charles Dickens writes in the opening lines as he paints a picture of life in England and France. Both countries were experiencing very similar and very different situations. In France, the class struggle was the cause of the French Revolution, but in England, the cause of the revolution was religious and spiritual.

The upper class used to have full rights and live the best of their times. They treated the mob as if they were animals without rights. The lower class suffered from hunger, need, and oppression — they were living the worst times. These conditions foreshadow the coming event (the French Revolution).

The year is late 1775. Jarvis Lorry travels from London to Paris on a secret mission for Tellson's Bank. Joining him is Lucie Manette, a 17-year-old woman who is stunned to learn that her father, Doctor Alexandre Manette, is alive and has recently been released after being secretly imprisoned in Paris for 18 years.
