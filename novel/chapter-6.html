📖 Chapter 6: Darnay's Love for Lucie

Darnay declares his love for Lucie and earns Dr. Manette's blessing.

📝 Summary
Chapters 5 & 6
  • A year passed. Darnay left all his property, his land, and even his title. He went to live in England where he earned his living by teaching French. He was a very successful teacher and became well known.
  • Darnay went to see Dr. Manette one summer evening when he knew that Lucie was out. He told Dr. Manette that he loved Lucie and wanted to marry her.
  • Darnay promised that he would never separate them because he knew how much they loved and depended on each other.
  • Darnay mentioned that he and Dr. Manette were similar to each other because both of them were wronged at the hands of the nobles, they both left France and went to England to earn their living, both became successful, and they both loved Lucie so much.
❓ Questions
  • How did Darnay earn his living in England?
  • What did Darnay promise Dr. Manette regarding Lucie?
  • How did Darnay try to show Dr. Manette that they were similar?
