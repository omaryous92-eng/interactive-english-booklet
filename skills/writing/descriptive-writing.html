🎨 Descriptive Writing

Sensory Details — use adjectives, similes, and metaphors to create vivid descriptions.

👁️ Sight 👂 Sound 👃 Smell 👅 Taste ✋ Touch

🧠 The 5 Senses

👁️
Sight
"The golden sun set behind the purple mountains."
👂
Sound
"The waves crashed against the shore like thunder."
👃
Smell
"The fresh scent of pine trees filled the air."
👅
Taste
"The sweet, tangy flavor of ripe mangoes."
✋
Touch
"The rough bark of the old oak tree."

🏗️ Structure

01
Title
Choose a simple, engaging title that gives a hint of what the description is about.
📌
02
Introduction (Setting the Scene)
Capture the reader's attention and set the tone. Introduce the topic, tell when and where it takes place.
🎬
03
Body Paragraphs (Details)
Break the body into 2–3 paragraphs. Use strong adjectives and similes/metaphors. Describe using the 5 senses.
📄
04
Conclusion (Wrap-up + Final Impression)
Leave the reader with a lasting image or thought. Summarize the overall impression and add a final sensory detail or emotion.
🏁

💡 Tips

  • Use the five senses: sight, sound, smell, taste, touch
  • Use strong adjectives (e.g., magnificent, tiny, sparkling)
  • Use similes and metaphors to create vivid images
  • Show, don't tell — describe the scene so the reader can imagine it
