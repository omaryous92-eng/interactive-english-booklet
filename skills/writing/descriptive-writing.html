🎨 Descriptive Writing

🏗️ Structure
Sensory Details
1. Title

Choose a simple, engaging title that gives a hint of what the description is about.

Examples: A Day at the Beach · My Grandfather's Garden · The Haunted House

2. Introduction Paragraph (Setting the Scene)

Capture the reader's attention and set the tone.

• Introduce the topic • Tell when and where it takes place

3. Body Paragraphs (Details)

Break the body into 2–3 paragraphs. Use strong adjectives and similes/metaphors.

4. Conclusion Paragraph (Wrap-up + Final Impression)

Leave the reader with a lasting image or thought.

• Summarize the overall impression • Add a final sensory detail or emotion

💡 Tips
  • Use the five senses: sight, sound, smell, taste, touch
  • Use strong adjectives (e.g., magnificent, tiny, sparkling)
  • Use similes and metaphors to create vivid images
  • Show, don't tell — describe the scene so the reader can imagine it
