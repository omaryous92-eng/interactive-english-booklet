📝 Opinion Essay

Structure & Rules — base your point of view on strong evidence and arguments.

📌 Introduction 📌 Body 📌 Conclusion
💡
Strong Evidence
Base your point of view on strong evidence and arguments.
🔄
State Your Position
You are free to not agree with the topics and state your position.
🔗
Logical Connectors
Use logical connectors throughout your essay so that your writing sounds smooth.

🏗️ Structure

01
Introduction
Present the topic and state your opinion clearly. Hook the reader with an interesting opening.
📌
02
Body Paragraph 1
First argument with evidence and examples. Use logical connectors to link your ideas.
📄
03
Body Paragraph 2
Second argument with evidence and examples. Build on your previous points.
📄
04
Body Paragraph 3 (Optional)
Third argument or counterargument. Address opposing views and explain why your position is stronger.
📄
05
Conclusion
Summarize your opinion and restate your position. Leave the reader with a final thought or call to action.
🏁
✏️
"All cars that burn fossil fuels should be banned and electric cars should replace them."

Do you agree or disagree? Write an opinion essay expressing your view with clear arguments.
