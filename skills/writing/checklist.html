✅ Writing Checklist

Use this checklist to review and improve your writing.

📌 Content 📐 Structure 📝 Language 🔍 Final Edit
📌 Content
  • ✓ Does my writing have a clear purpose?
  • ✓ Did I stay on topic throughout?
  • ✓ Did I include enough details and examples?
  • ✓ Is my opinion or main idea clearly stated?
📐 Structure
  • ✓ Does my writing have a clear beginning, middle, and end?
  • ✓ Are my paragraphs organized logically?
  • ✓ Did I use transition words (first, next, however, therefore)?
  • ✓ Is my conclusion satisfying?
📝 Language
  • ✓ Did I use a variety of sentence structures?
  • ✓ Did I use powerful verbs and adjectives?
  • ✓ Did I avoid repetition of words?
  • ✓ Did I use correct grammar and punctuation?
🔍 Final Edit
  • ✓ Did I check my spelling?
  • ✓ Did I check my punctuation (.,!?;\"\")?
  • ✓ Did I read my work aloud to check for flow?
  • ✓ Did I ask someone else to read it?

🤝 Peer Review Questions

  • What is the main idea of this piece?
  • What is the strongest part of this writing?
  • What could be improved?
  • Is the writing clear and easy to understand?
