📖 Narrative Writing

✅ Narrative Writing Checklist
Review

Beginning

  • ✓ I have included a "hook" to grab the reader's attention
  • ✓ I have described my setting
  • ✓ I have introduced story characters

Middle

  • ✓ I have used the 5 senses to create atmosphere
  • ✓ I have included 'action'
  • ✓ I have introduced a problem or event

Ending

  • ✓ I have tried to resolve the problem
  • ✓ I have used powerful verbs and adjectives
  • ✓ I have included dialogue

Editing

  • ✓ I have checked my spelling and punctuation
  • ✓ I have used a thesaurus to find synonyms
  • ✓ I have checked my story makes sense
🗺️ Map the Story — Example

Story: Mila wanted to ride her bike. Her mother told her to put on her knee pads. Mila didn't like to wear knee pads, so she didn't put them on. While riding down the sidewalk, Mila fell off of her bike and scraped her knee. She went home and apologized to her mom, promising to wear her pads from now on.

What happened first?

Mila wanted to ride her bike.

What happened second?

Her mother told her to wear knee pads.

What happened third?

She fell off her bike and scraped her knee.

How did the story end?

She apologized and promised to wear her knee pads.
