💬 Dialogue 4: Newspapers & Magazines

A: Why do you read newspapers or magazines?
B: Newspapers/magazines can broaden your mind about thousands of things without the need to travel.
A: How often do you read them?
B: I read it every day.
A: When is the best time to read them?
B: I think the best time is in the morning when you start a new day.
A: What do you usually read about?
B: I usually read politics and fashion magazines. I also enjoy reading about cultures.
A: What is your favorite newspaper?
B: My favorite is The New York Times, which writes about all sorts of things: business, sports, movies, travel, books, jobs, education, and real estate.
A: Do you prefer online news or printed newspapers?
B: I prefer online news to save money.
A: How much does it cost you every month?
B: I only buy a monthly newspaper, so it doesn't cost much money. Around $5/month.
A: What is the most reliable magazine in your opinion?
B: I think it would be Forbes magazine, a leading source for reliable business news and financial information.
A: Do you think newspapers will disappear one day?
B: Yes, unfortunately it's just a matter of time.
