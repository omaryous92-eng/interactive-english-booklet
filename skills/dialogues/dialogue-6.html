💬 Dialogue 6: Hotels

A: What is your favorite hotel? Where is it located?
B: My favorite hotel is the Burj Al Arab in Dubai, UAE.
A: How do you know that hotel?
B: I stayed there during my vacation last year.
A: Does it attract many tourists?
B: Yes, it's one of the most famous hotels in the world and attracts thousands of tourists every year.
A: What type of room did you stay in and what facilities did you get from the hotel?
B: I stayed in a suite with a sea view. The hotel had a pool, spa, gym, and several restaurants.
A: What do you like about that hotel?
B: The service is excellent and the view is breathtaking.
A: Are all the staff friendly and helpful?
B: Yes, the staff are very professional and welcoming.
A: How much does it cost a night?
B: It is very expensive, costing over $1,000 per night.
A: Do you recommend that hotel to friends?
B: Absolutely! It's a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
