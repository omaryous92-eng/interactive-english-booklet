💬 Dialogue 5: Movies

A: Do you usually watch movies at home or at a movie theater?
B: I prefer watching movies at home because it's more comfortable.
A: Is watching movies at the theater more interesting than watching movies at home?
B: Yes, the big screen and sound effects make it more exciting.
A: How often do you go to the movie theater?
B: About once a month, when there's a movie I really want to see.
A: What's your favorite type of movie? What movie of that type do you like?
B: I love action movies. My favorite is Avengers: Endgame.
A: Was that movie adapted from a book?
B: No, it was originally written for the screen.
A: Who are the actors/actresses in the movie?
B: Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Scarlett Johansson, and many more.
