🌅 "My Heart Leaps Up"

by William Wordsworth

📜 The Poem
Romantic
My heart leaps up when I behold A rainbow in the sky; So was it when my life began; So is it now I am a man; So be it when I shall grow old, Or let me die! The Child is father of the Man; And I could wish my days to be Bound each to each by natural piety.
📖 Vocabulary
Rainbow
an arch of different colours that sometimes appears in the sky opposite the sun after rain
Leaps
jumps
Behold
see
Bound
joined together
Piety
the showing and the feeling of deep respect for God
📝 Paraphrase
Explanation

In these lines, the poet is describing his happiness when he sees a rainbow in the sky. The poet's love for nature is reflected in these lines. He talks about himself in connection with nature. He says we acquire our love for nature as we grow up. He compares the continuity of this natural phenomenon (rainbow) to the continuity of man's life.

The seventh line sums up this idea with a kind of philosophy: "The Child is father of the Man."

In the last two lines, the poet sums up his love for nature saying: I wish my days to be linked together in harmony with the beauty of nature and love and respect for God. As long as he remembers nature, he feels happy.

🔍 Literary Terms
1. Personification

"My heart leaps up when I behold"

The poet gives the heart a human feature and depicts it as a man who can jump with joy. The poet is comparing his happiness when he sees the rainbow to a person jumping through the air.

2. Repetition

"So was it when my life began; / So is it now I am a man; / So be it when I shall grow old,"

The word "so" is repeated three times.

3. Contrast

There is a contrast in the meaning between:
"So was it when my life began; / So is it now I am a man; / So be it when I shall grow old, / Or let me die!"

4. Metaphor

"And I could wish my days to be / Bound each to each by natural piety"

The poet gives life the image of a chain which is joined.

5. Paradox

"The Child is father of the Man"

The poet uses a statement which seems to be self-contradictory and absurd, yet turns out to have a reasonable and sensible meaning.

💡 Notes & Analysis
Theme
  • The poet describes the beauty of the rainbow and the cycle of life.
  • The poet sums up his philosophy of life. He believes that every human being is influenced by his childhood and that the child is man's father.
  • The rainbow represented the cycle of life.
  • The poet compares a man's life that ends to the rainbow that never ends as it is eternal.
  • The poet's wish for life is to live happily with the beauty of nature in respect to God.
  • He thinks that life is like a cycle, like the rainbow, and we should spend our lives in respect to God.
