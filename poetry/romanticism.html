🎨 Romanticism

The Romantic era in English Literature — an artistic and literary movement.

📜 What is Romanticism?
18th-19th Century

Romanticism (also called the Romantic era or Romantic period) is an artistic and literary movement that was:

  • A reaction to the Industrial Revolution
  • A revolt against formal rules and traditions
  • A rejection to the age of reason

Romanticism swept away philosophy, laws, science, and formulas.

📝 William Wordsworth
1770–1850
📜

Major English Romantic Poet

William Wordsworth (7 April 1770 – 23 April 1850) was a major English Romantic poet who helped to launch the Romantic Age in English Literature.

Born in his beloved Lake District, he was the son of an attorney. He studied at St John's College, Cambridge. In 1790, he went on a walking tour to France, the Alps, and Italy.

💡 Wordsworth's Concept of Poetry

"A spontaneous overflow of powerful feelings and emotions recollected in tranquility."

— William Wordsworth
