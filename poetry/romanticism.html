📜 What is Romanticism?

An artistic and literary movement that changed the world

🏭
Reaction to Industrial Revolution

Romanticism emerged as a response to the rapid industrialization and urbanization of society. Poets sought refuge in nature and the natural world.

📜
Revolt Against Tradition

A rejection of formal rules, traditions, and the strict structures of classical poetry. Romantic poets valued freedom of expression.

🧠
Rejection of Reason

A rejection of the Age of Reason (Enlightenment). Romanticism emphasized emotion, intuition, and the power of the human imagination.

📝 William Wordsworth

The father of Romantic poetry in English literature

📜
William Wordsworth
📅 1770 – 1850

William Wordsworth was a major English Romantic poet who helped to launch the Romantic Age in English Literature. Born in his beloved Lake District, he studied at St John's College, Cambridge. In 1790, he went on a walking tour to France, the Alps, and Italy.

Lake District Romantic Poet Nature Lover
